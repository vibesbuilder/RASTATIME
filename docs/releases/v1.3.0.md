## RASTATIME v1.3.0 (2026-11-07)

A release on top of 1.2.0: show boundaries that keep the clock, a
yearly people count that follows persons instead of episodes, a faster
library and fixes around concurrent scheduling and a stale browser tab.
No database migration.

### Changed

- **Strict show boundaries.**  A show now starts on its scheduled time,
  never earlier and never later:
  - Before a show **without** *Allow delayed start* the previous track
    ends at the boundary (a short 0.3 s fade) instead of playing its
    tail up to ~4.5 s into the next show.  A show **with** *Allow
    delayed start* keeps the old behaviour and lets the previous track
    finish.
  - When the previous show's items end a few seconds before the
    boundary, the gap stays silent and the new show starts on its row
    instead of moving up (the 00:00 case: 3 s early before).
  - Both are measured on the schedule rows plus the measured chain lag,
    not on the engine's queue (which runs ~3 s ahead of the air).
  - Within a show playback stays gapless.  Shows that rely on running
    over into the next one should tick *Allow delayed start* on the
    show that follows.
- **People Involved counts persons.**  Analytics → People Involved is
  the figure for the yearly statistics:
  - The show field is now **Hosts involved** (was *Number of involved
    people*) and counts once per show and year, however many episodes
    aired.
  - The Archive Online field is now **Guests involved** (was *People
    involved*) and adds up per episode on top of the hosts.  Before, an
    episode value replaced the show value.
  - People involved = hosts + guests.  *Repeat of* shows are listed but
    count nothing, because their people were counted with the original.
  - The page and the CSV show hosts, guests and people per show.
- **Faster library.**
  - Opening the library takes about half the time.  Covers are served
    as cacheable images instead of being embedded in every response.
  - Deleting many tracks no longer takes ~2 s per file, and a busy state
    shows while it runs.
- **Apache: warm PHP pool (recommended).**  `INSTALL_APACHE.md` now
  sets `FcgidMinProcessesPerClass 8` and `FcgidIdleTimeout 300`.
  Without them, bursts of parallel requests are served in 1-second
  steps.

### Fixed

- **Auto DJ and manual scheduling no longer deadlock.**  The automatic
  fill of a show and an operator editing the same show could block
  each other on the database.  Every scheduling transaction now locks
  the show instance first.  The automatic fill skips a show that is
  being edited and comes back on the next pass.
- **A stale browser tab no longer locks the operator out.**  A calendar
  tab with an outdated security token produced a series of refusals,
  and the firewall watcher blocked the operator's address.  Such a
  request now gets a distinct answer that the watcher ignores, and the
  browser stops polling.
- **Smart block:** the minutes field of *Exclude tracks / artists
  played lately* now appears in every open smart block dialog, not
  only the first.
- **Start page:** the weekly programme and player widgets keep light
  text on the photo in browsers set to light mode.

### Upgrading from 1.2.0

Follow `docs/operator/UPGRADE.md`.  In short:

1. Make a backup (*Settings → Backup*).
2. Stop the RASTATIME services.  The running analyzer locks its
   executable, and a reinstall with the services running fails
   halfway.
3. Update the checkout and reinstall the five backend packages
   (`pip install -e`) so the services report 1.3.0.  There is **no
   database migration** in this release.
4. Start the services in order (`docs/operator/RESTART_ORDER.md`):
   - Start **the API before PLAYOUT**: PLAYOUT reads *Allow delayed
     start* from the API, and without it every boundary is treated as
     strict.
   - PLAYOUT renders a new Liquidsoap script (show transition with cut
     and hold), so `RASTATIME-LIQUIDSOAP` restarts as well.  Do not
     restart it by hand on top.
   - Restart Apache so it loads the new translations.
5. Optional: apply the two `mod_fcgid` lines from `INSTALL_APACHE.md`
   and restart Apache.

### Known limitations

- Windows only.
- `RASTATIME-WORKER` runs from the source tree.  It has no wheel among
  the release files.
- People Involved counts numbers, not names: a person who hosts two
  shows counts twice.

The full change history is in `CHANGELOG.md` (German).
